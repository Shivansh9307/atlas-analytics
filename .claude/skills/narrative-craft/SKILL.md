# Narrative craft

Guidance only. Write for the **named decision owner**, not a general audience.

## Answer first (pyramid)
Lead with the one-sentence answer. Then 2–4 supporting pillars. Then evidence under
each. The reader should be able to stop after sentence one and still be right.

## SCQA for the setup
**Situation** (what we knew) → **Complication** (what changed/broke) →
**Question** (the decision) → **Answer** (your finding). Keep S and C to a line each.

## Claims, not labels — every title
A slide title is an assertion the evidence supports, not a topic.
- ❌ "EMEA Margin Analysis"  ✅ "EMEA margin fell 4pts because volume shifted to Hardware"
- ❌ "Revenue by Segment"    ✅ "Two segments drove 80% of the revenue miss"
If a title has no verb and no number, it is probably a label — rewrite it.

## Discipline
- Every number carries a provenance ID `[c_xxx]` that resolves in the ledger.
- Name the evidence tier when it matters (decomposed vs correlational).
- Cut adjectives; keep the number. "materially lower" → "4.0pts lower".
- End on the decision: what should the owner do Monday?
